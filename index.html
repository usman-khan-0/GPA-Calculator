What is a GPA?

Your Grade Point Average is a weighted average of the grade points you earn in each class. Courses with more credit hours have a larger effect on the final number.

Should I use the standard or weighted scale?

Use the scale your school uses. Standard scales cap at 4.0; weighted scales give additional points to advanced, honors, or AP courses.

Does GPAwise save my grades?

Your entries are saved only in your browser so you can come back to them. Nothing is sent to a server or connected to an account.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. GPAwise is designed to work comfortably on phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
